## Executive Overview

The Q1 Strategy Review is EDGE Platform's most consequential internal event of the year — the moment where data meets decision. Senior leadership, department heads, and key stakeholders convene to pressure-test assumptions, surface structural gaps, and lock in the organizational priorities that will govern the next three quarters.

This is a working session. Attendees leave with concrete commitments, not slide decks.

## Format & Modality

This event runs as a **hybrid experience**. In-person attendees join at Hilton London Bankside. Remote participants access the strategic sessions via a secured virtual room with interactive participation tools, live polling, and breakout room access.

Breakfast and lunch are provided for in-person attendees. Dress code is business casual.

## Who Should Attend

This is a **members-only, invite-only event** for:

- Senior managers and department heads
- C-suite and VP-level stakeholders
- Key individual contributors involved in strategic planning cycles
- Board observers by invitation of the Chair

Registration is mandatory. Requests for virtual access must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance.

## Prerequisites

Attendees are expected to arrive having reviewed the Q4 2023 performance report distributed on 1 March 2024, and having completed the pre-session department briefing template shared via the leadership portal.
