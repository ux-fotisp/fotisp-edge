## Executive Overview

The Digital Transformation Summit 2024 gathered over 300 senior executives, technology architects, and innovation leaders at the Metropolitan Expo in Athens for a full-day deep dive into enterprise modernization.

From AI deployment frameworks to cloud infrastructure governance, attendees left with concrete, actionable strategies validated by organizations that have already completed large-scale digital transitions.

## Why Attend

Whether you are six months into a transformation program or still mapping your strategic roadmap, this summit delivers peer-validated frameworks, not vendor pitches. Every session is governed by a strict no-sales-deck policy.

## What to Expect

Three parallel workshop tracks run throughout the afternoon, allowing attendees to self-select their deep-dive focus:

- **Track A: Cloud Architecture** — Infrastructure patterns for scale
- **Track B: AI & Automation** — From proof-of-concept to production
- **Track C: Organizational Change** — Adoption strategies that stick

## Target Audience

Senior managers, CTOs, digital transformation leads, enterprise architects, and innovation officers from organizations with 200+ employees actively engaged in or planning a digital transformation initiative.
