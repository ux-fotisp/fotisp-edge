Strategy is not a document — it's a set of clear choices about where to compete, how to win, and what to stop doing. Our Strategic Planning service helps leadership teams move from ambiguity to clarity, and from clarity to execution.

## The Strategic Planning Process

### Phase 1: Situation Analysis (Weeks 1–3)
- Market analysis and competitive landscape assessment
- Internal capabilities and resource audit
- Customer and stakeholder research
- SWOT synthesis and strategic issue identification

### Phase 2: Strategic Direction (Weeks 4–6)
- Vision and mission refinement workshops
- Strategic options generation and evaluation
- Priority setting with leadership team
- 3–5 year strategic direction definition

### Phase 3: Strategic Plan Development (Weeks 7–9)
- Objectives and key results (OKR) framework
- Initiative roadmap with ownership and timelines
- Resource allocation and investment planning
- Risk identification and mitigation planning

### Phase 4: Activation & Governance (Ongoing)
- Quarterly strategy review facilitation
- KPI dashboard development
- Mid-year strategy refresh workshops
- Annual planning cycle support

## Engagement Models

We work with clients on fixed-fee project engagements for annual planning cycles, and on retainer for ongoing strategic advisory support throughout the year.
