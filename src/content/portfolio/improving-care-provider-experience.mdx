## The Challenge

Care providers were overwhelmed by fragmented software interfaces when managing patient records and claims.

## Our Approach

We consolidated 4 disparate provider web tools into a single, accessible portal interface.

## The Results

- **70% reduction** in claim processing errors
- Provider satisfaction score increased from 42 to 88
