## The Challenge

A regional financial services group with over 200 branches relied on a combination of spreadsheets, email chains, and weekly phone calls to manage branch-level performance reporting. Branch managers spent 4–6 hours per week on administrative tasks that added no customer value.

## Our Approach

We conducted a two-week discovery phase interviewing 24 branch managers across 6 regions to understand their actual workflow, pain points, and information needs.

Key findings:
- Managers needed real-time visibility into 11 core KPIs, but existing systems required manual data aggregation
- Most performance conversations happened reactively (end of month) rather than proactively
- Mobile access was critical — managers are rarely at their desks

## The Solution

We designed and built a React Native mobile application connected to the group's existing data warehouse via a secure API layer. Key features:

- **Live dashboard** — 11 configurable KPI widgets updating every 15 minutes
- **Alerts & nudges** — Push notifications when a branch metric falls outside target range
- **Team messaging** — Secure in-app channel for manager-to-regional director communication
- **Goal tracking** — Monthly target setting and progress visualization
- **Offline mode** — Core data accessible without network connection

## The Results

- **4.5 hours per week** saved per manager on administrative tasks
- **67% reduction** in end-of-month performance surprises
- **91% adoption rate** within 90 days of launch
- App Store rating: **4.8/5**
