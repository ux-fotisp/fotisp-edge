## The Challenge

A global logistics company had petabytes of operational data but lacked real-time insights to optimize delivery routes.

## Our Approach

We built an automated predictive analytics pipeline using machine learning on Cloudflare Workers and D1.

## The Results

- **18% reduction** in fuel consumption
- **94% on-time delivery rate** achieved across all European hubs
