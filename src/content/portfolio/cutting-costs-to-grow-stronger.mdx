## The Challenge

A leading consumer goods company was struggling with rising operational costs that were eroding margins and limiting investment in product innovation. Despite strong revenue, profitability was declining year-over-year.

## Our Approach

We began with a comprehensive operational audit covering procurement, logistics, manufacturing overhead, and organizational structure. Our team identified three key areas of opportunity:

1. **Supply chain consolidation** — The company was working with 47 tier-1 suppliers, many of whom provided overlapping capabilities at higher-than-market rates.
2. **Warehouse network optimization** — An outdated 12-location warehouse network was over-indexed for the company's current distribution footprint.
3. **Shared services implementation** — Finance, HR, and legal functions were duplicated across business units.

## The Results

Over 18 months of implementation:

- **31% reduction** in operational overhead
- **12 supplier relationships** consolidated into 3 preferred partnerships
- **4 warehouse locations** closed, with fulfillment re-routed to 3 upgraded hubs
- **€4.2M annual savings** reinvested into R&D

The company used these savings to fund the development of two new product lines, both of which achieved top-3 market share positions within their first year.
