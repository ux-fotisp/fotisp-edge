A legacy print media brand was experiencing declining subscription revenue and advertising revenue as audiences migrated online.

## Our Approach

We designed a digital-first editorial and commercial strategy, creating new subscription models and modern ad formats.

## The Results

- **45% increase** in digital subscriptions in year 1
- Successful launch of 2 premium podcast channels
