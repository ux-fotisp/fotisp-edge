## The Challenge

A global media conglomerate with 14 distinct brands across print, digital, and broadcast had grown through acquisition without a coherent portfolio strategy. Each brand operated independently, creating costly duplication and missed cross-selling opportunities.

## Our Approach

We conducted a thorough portfolio analysis using our proprietary brand mapping framework, assessing each property on two dimensions: market attractiveness and competitive differentiation.

Based on this analysis, we recommended:
- **Invest & grow** — 4 brands with strong digital audiences and monetization potential
- **Harvest** — 6 mature brands generating steady cash flow requiring minimal investment
- **Divest or shut down** — 4 brands consuming resources with limited strategic fit

## Execution

We worked alongside the group's executive team for 8 months to:
- Redesign the group's organizational structure around the new portfolio logic
- Establish shared technology infrastructure across the "invest" brands
- Negotiate the sale of two divested properties to strategic buyers
- Develop an integrated content distribution strategy leveraging cross-brand audiences

## The Results

- **€8.1M in annual costs eliminated** through shared services and platform consolidation
- **22% increase** in digital audience across the core brand portfolio within 12 months
- **Two successful divestitures** completed at premium valuations
