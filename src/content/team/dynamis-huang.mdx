Dynamis Huang directs EDGE's advanced cryptographic research lab, specialising in post-quantum key exchange mechanisms, lattice-based encryption, and zero-knowledge proof acceleration on edge compute nodes.

Dynamis holds a PhD in Applied Cryptography from ETH Zürich and has pioneered zero-overhead attestation layers for multi-tenant micro-runtimes.

### Research Imperative

> "Security cannot be a post-facto wrapper. It must be woven into the algebraic fabric of data execution."

He works closely with international standards bodies and open-source cryptographic workgroups to harden next-generation web infrastructure against quantum adversary models.
