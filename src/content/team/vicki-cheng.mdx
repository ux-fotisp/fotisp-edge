Vicki is a Senior Partner with over 18 years of consulting experience focused on organizational transformation, leadership development, and people strategy. She works primarily with large organizations navigating significant change — whether driven by growth, restructuring, or digital transformation.

She is known for her ability to work effectively across cultures and organizational levels, having led engagements in 14 countries across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and North America.

Vicki's recent work includes designing the leadership development programme for a 15,000-person logistics company, advising on the people strategy for a high-profile cross-border merger, and leading a global talent benchmarking study for a professional services firm.
