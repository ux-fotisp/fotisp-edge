Elena serves as Chief Technology Officer at EDGE, leading the engineering division in developing zero-trust micro-architectures and real-time distributed telemetry systems.

With over 16 years of experience in high-throughput systems, kernel-level optimization, and quantum-resistant cryptography, she leads the technical direction across EDGE's global edge nodes.

Prior to joining EDGE, Elena was VP of Engineering at a cyber-defense scaleup and holds a Master's degree in Computer Science from MIT.
