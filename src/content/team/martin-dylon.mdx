Martin has over 20 years of financial leadership experience spanning multiple continents and industries including financial services, consumer goods, and professional services.

He specializes in strategic financial planning, mergers & acquisitions, capital markets, and building finance functions that serve as genuine business partners rather than cost centers.

Before joining EDGE, Martin held CFO positions at leading firms in the UK and US financial sectors. He has led two successful IPOs, multiple debt financing rounds, and three cross-border acquisitions.
