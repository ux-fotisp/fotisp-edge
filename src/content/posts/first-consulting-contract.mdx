Breaking into consulting is one of the most rewarding career moves you can make, but landing that first contract can feel overwhelming. Here are six proven steps to help you close your first deal.

## 1. Define Your Niche

The most successful consultants specialize. Identify the specific problems you solve and the industries you understand best. A generalist struggles to compete; a specialist commands premium rates.

## 2. Build a Credibility Portfolio

Even without paid clients, you can demonstrate expertise through:
- Published case studies or retrospectives
- Open-source contributions or side projects
- Pro-bono work for nonprofits
- Thought leadership content (articles, talks)

## 3. Leverage Your Existing Network

Your first clients will almost always come from people who already know, like, and trust you. Make a list of former colleagues, managers, and contacts who might need your skills — and reach out authentically.

## 4. Price Confidently

Avoid underpricing — it signals low value. Research market rates for your expertise level and geography. Consider a day rate or project-based fee structure rather than hourly to avoid commoditization.

## 5. Create a Simple Proposal Template

A clear, professional proposal shows prospects you are organized and serious. Include:
- The problem you're solving
- Your proposed approach
- Deliverables and timeline
- Investment (fee)
- Next steps

## 6. Follow Up Consistently

Most deals require 5–8 touchpoints before a decision. Create a simple CRM (even a spreadsheet) to track your pipeline and follow up on a schedule. Persistence, not pushiness, wins contracts.
