The venture capital landscape has shifted significantly over the past several years. After the frothy conditions of 2020–2021 and the subsequent correction, a new set of rules governs how deals are sourced, evaluated, and closed.

## Rule 1: Revenue Traction Matters Again

The "growth at all costs" era is over. Investors are back to scrutinizing unit economics, payback periods, and paths to profitability before writing cheques.

## Rule 2: The Bar for Series A Has Moved

What used to get a seed round now gets a pre-seed. What used to get a Series A now requires Series A+ traction. Adjust your fundraising expectations accordingly.

## Rule 3: Relationships Still Win

The best deals still come through warm introductions. Cold outreach conversion rates are low. Spend time building relationships with investors before you need to fundraise.

## Rule 4: Down Rounds Are Real

Companies that raised at inflated 2021 valuations face difficult choices. Flat rounds and structured deals are increasingly common. Don't let pride prevent you from accepting necessary capital.

## Rule 5: The LP Cycle Matters

VCs are constrained by their own fundraising cycles. If a firm hasn't closed a new fund recently, their deployment pace slows. Understanding your target investor's fund lifecycle helps you time outreach.

## The Bottom Line

Venture capital is still an option for the right businesses — but the rules of engagement have normalized. Build a business that could succeed without VC, and you'll be in a much stronger position when you seek it.
