The fastest-growing companies don't guess what customers want — they build systematic feedback loops that directly fuel product and marketing decisions. This approach, often called "growth hacking," is less about clever tricks and more about ruthless prioritization driven by real user data.

## The Foundation: Talk to Customers

Before any tool or framework, the most effective growth practice is direct conversation. Conduct 30-minute interviews with your best customers. Ask:

- What problem were you trying to solve when you found us?
- What almost stopped you from purchasing?
- How would you describe us to a friend?

The language customers use to describe your product is gold for copywriting, positioning, and SEO.

## Build a Feedback Infrastructure

Systematic feedback beats ad-hoc conversations. Implement:

1. **NPS surveys** — Quarterly, automated, short
2. **In-product micro-surveys** — Contextual, one question at key moments
3. **Support ticket analysis** — Mine recurring themes monthly
4. **Churn interviews** — Call every churned customer who'll talk

## Close the Loop

Feedback that doesn't drive action erodes trust. When customers report an issue or request a feature, close the loop when you act on it. A simple email — "We heard you, and here's what we built" — builds enormous goodwill.

## The Growth Flywheel

Feedback → insight → product improvement → better retention → more referrals → more customers → more feedback. This flywheel, once turning, is the most durable growth engine in business.
