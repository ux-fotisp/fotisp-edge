Every manual process is a liability. It consumes time, introduces human error, and scales poorly. Yet most organizations dramatically underinvest in automation — not because they don't see its value, but because they don't know where to start.

## Step 1: Audit Your Manual Workflows

Spend one week documenting every recurring manual task across your team. For each, record:
- Frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Time cost per occurrence
- Error rate
- Who performs it

## Step 2: Prioritize by ROI

Not all automation is equal. Calculate a simple ROI score:

`ROI = (Time saved per month × hourly cost) ÷ automation build cost`

Target processes with ROI > 1 within 6 months.

## Step 3: Build Versus Buy

For most processes, off-the-shelf tools (Zapier, Make, n8n) are faster and cheaper than custom development. Reserve custom builds for processes with complex logic or competitive differentiation.

## Common High-ROI Automations

- **Lead qualification** — Enrich and score inbound leads automatically
- **Invoice processing** — OCR + accounting software integrations
- **Report generation** — Schedule and distribute weekly KPI reports
- **Customer onboarding** — Triggered email sequences and task creation
- **Data synchronization** — Keep CRM, ERP, and support tools in sync

## The Human Factor

Automation frees people from repetitive work — it doesn't replace judgment, creativity, or relationship-building. Communicate this clearly during rollout to reduce resistance and maximize adoption.
